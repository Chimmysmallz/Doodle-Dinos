Privacy Policy — Doodle Dinos

Effective date: [Add date]  ·  Last updated: [Add date]

The short version: Doodle Dinos does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app runs fully offline on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

Doodle Dinos ("the app", "we", "us") is an educational play app made for young children (ages 1–6). We are committed to protecting the privacy of every family that uses it. This policy explains what the app does — and, more importantly, what it does not do — with information.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, request, or transmit any personal or sensitive information, including (but not limited to): names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, microphone audio, precise or approximate location, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. The app has no sign-in and no user accounts.

2. Information stored on the device

To remember your preferences between sessions, the app saves a few simple settings locally on the device only (using the device's local storage): chosen language, voice, sound on/off, the star count, and whether premium has been unlocked. This information never leaves the device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. No ads and no third-party services

The app contains no advertising, no third-party advertising networks, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no social-media integrations.

4. Permissions and internet

The app includes no internet permission — it cannot send or receive data over a network. Spoken audio (letters, animals, stories) is produced on the device using its built-in text-to-speech, or from audio files bundled inside the app; nothing is streamed.

The only permission the app may request is the microphone, and only if you choose to use the optional "Parent Voice" feature (see below). If you never use that feature, the microphone is never accessed.

5. Optional "Parent Voice" feature

Premium users may record their own voice reading the bedtime stories so a child can hear them at bedtime. These recordings are created and stored only on your device (in the app's local storage) and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or any third party. You can delete a recording at any time from within the app, and all recordings are removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

5. Children's privacy

Doodle Dinos is designed for children and complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / "GDPR-K"), and Google Play's Families policy. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. In-app purchases

[Keep this section only if you offer real paid purchases.] If optional premium content is offered for purchase, all payments are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive, or store your payment details. Google's handling of payment information is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain, share, or delete on any server. Any settings saved on your device can be removed at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
