## Full description (max 4000 characters)

**Doodle Dinos is a safe, joyful playground for little ones aged 1–6 — the app you can hand over without hovering.**

No ads. No internet needed. No data collected. Just bright, friendly activities that let toddlers and preschoolers create, learn, play, and wind down — while you get a few minutes back.

🦕 **A whole playground in one app**
• 🎨 Doodle Pad — finger-paint with big colors and a rainbow brush
• 🎹 Music Pads — tap happy, colorful notes
• 🔤 ABC & 123 — letters and numbers spoken aloud
• 🐮 Animal Sounds — tap an animal, hear its name and sound
• 🔵 Shapes & Colors — gentle early concepts
• 🫧 Pop Bubbles — calm, no-lose tapping
• 🔎 Find It! — a simple, cheerful matching game
• 🌙 Bedtime — soothing lullabies and read-aloud story cards
• ⭐ Stars — little rewards that keep the smiles coming

👶 **Grows with your child**
One tap switches between **Little Ones (1–3)** — big, tap-anywhere, open-ended play — and **Big Kids (4–6)** — letters, numbers, and gentle games with simple instructions.

🔒 **Built for parents' peace of mind**
• **No ads, ever** — no pop-ups, no banners
• **No internet required** — everything runs on the device
• **No data collected** — no accounts, no tracking
• **Parent gate** — settings are locked behind a quick grown-ups-only check
• **No fail states** — every activity encourages and rewards

🌍 **Seven languages**
English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Igbo, Yoruba, and Hausa — menus and settings adapt instantly.

🗣️ **Friendly voices & calm sounds**
Choose from soft, kid-friendly voices, with a gentle bedtime mode for winding down.

💛 **Why families love Doodle Dinos**
It respects your child and your trust: thoughtfully designed, distraction-free, and genuinely calm. Perfect for quiet time at home, car rides, waiting rooms, and bedtime.

Download Doodle Dinos and give your little one a safe, happy place to play. 🦕✨
