# Doodle Dinos: End-to-End Test Checklist

Work through each section on your phone and tick each line. Write down anything that looks or sounds wrong, with the screen name, so it is easy to fix.

**Before you start:** turn the phone volume up, turn off silent mode, and tap anything once on the home screen (phones keep sound off until the first tap).

## 1. Home screen
- [ ] Doodle the dino waves next to the title. Tapping him makes him hop and say hello.
- [ ] Every card shows its own dino picture (no empty boxes).
- [ ] **Little Ones · 1–3** hides ABC & 123, Spell It and Find It. **Big Kids · 4–6** shows them.
- [ ] The Memory and Counting cards show a lock while Premium is off.

## 2. Original activities
- [ ] **Doodle Pad:** drawing works with a finger. The rainbow brush, eraser and bin work.
- [ ] **Music Pads:** each pad plays a note.
- [ ] **ABC & 123:** tapping F says "F. Fish" (not "F, S"). Numbers 1 to 10 work.
- [ ] **Animal Sounds:** each animal is named (real sounds play only if you added clips to `audio/`).
- [ ] **Shapes & Colors**, **Pop Bubbles** and **Find It!** all respond and award stars.
- [ ] **Bedtime:** stories read aloud, ◀ ▶ change story, the lullaby buttons play and stop.

## 3. New activities
- [ ] **Trace Letters:** a dotted letter with a pulsing green start dot. Tracing over the letter earns a star, a celebration and moves to the next letter. Scribbling elsewhere does not count. The capitals, small letters and numbers buttons all work.
- [ ] **Spell It:** Doodle spells the word first. Tapping the right letters fills the boxes. A wrong letter wiggles. In Little Ones mode, the next letter always glows.
- [ ] **Point at It:** Doodle asks "Can you point at the …?". A wrong tap names that picture. The five topic buttons change the pictures. Little Ones see 2 pictures, Big Kids see 4.
- [ ] **Sing Along:** a 3-2-1 countdown, then the tune plays and each word lights up. The 🐢 button slows it down. Mary, Itsy Bitsy and Old MacDonald are locked while Premium is off.
- [ ] **Read Along:** "Read to me" reads each page and highlights the words, then turns the page by itself. Tapping any word says it. The last page shows "The End!". Goldilocks, Little Red Hen and Gingerbread Man are locked while Premium is off.
- [ ] **Sticker Book:** a "New sticker!" banner appeared after trying a new activity. The book shows the stickers you have earned and grey ones still to collect.

## 4. Parent tools (Settings, then answer the maths question)
- [ ] **Language:** switch to Yorùbá, then Igbo, then Hausa. The card names change. Switch back to English.
- [ ] **Voice:** each of the four voices plays when tapped.
- [ ] **Screen-time limit:** choose **10 min**. A "⏳ 10 min left" chip appears on the home screen and counts down. At 1 minute, Doodle says "One more minute!". At zero, the sleepy "Time for a break!" screen covers the app. **Grown-ups**, then the maths question, then **+5 min**, lets play continue. Then set the limit back to **Off**.
- [ ] **Keep your child in the app:** the pinning steps open. (The **Pin the app now** button only appears in the Android app, not on the website.)
- [ ] **Weekly summary:** shows minutes today and this week, a 7-day chart, favourite activities, letters traced and words spelled.
- [ ] **My Stories:** write a short story with two paragraphs, pick a picture, keep **Also read it at bedtime** ticked, and save. It appears in Read Along with a "From home" badge, and in Bedtime.

## 5. Premium (demo, no real payment)
- [ ] **Premium**, then **Unlock**, then the maths question, then choose a plan and confirm. The "What you unlocked!" screen appears.
- [ ] Memory and Counting open. All songs and books unlock. Bedtime has 9 stories plus your own.
- [ ] The profile chip appears. Add a second child: their sticker book and summary start empty. Switching back restores the first child's.
- [ ] **My Voice** (Safari tab on iPhone): record a story, play it back, turn on **Use my voice for stories**, and check Bedtime plays your voice. Your own stories from My Stories are in the list too.
- [ ] In a Read Along story you wrote and recorded, the 🎙️ button plays your recording.

## 6. Safety checks
- [ ] Turn on Airplane mode, close the app and reopen it. Everything still works.
- [ ] There are no ads, pop-ups or links that leave the app.
- [ ] A child cannot reach Settings, Premium or the time-up controls without the maths question.

## Known limits on the website version
- iPhone home-screen apps may block the microphone. Record in the Safari tab instead.
- Recordings made in the Safari tab may not show in the home-screen app, because iPhone keeps their storage separate.
- One-tap pinning is Android-app only. On iPhone use Guided Access (Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access).
